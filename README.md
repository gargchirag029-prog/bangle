# Maison Aura & Karat — The Bangle Chronicles
### Haute Joaillerie & Bespoke Bangle Customizer E-Commerce Boutique

A high-jewelry online boutique celebrating the sacred heritage of royal goldsmithing, Syndicate uncut polki diamonds, Jaipur kiln champlevé meenakari, and modern architectural wrist stacks.

---

## ✨ Features & Architecture

### 1. Haute Joaillerie Design Language
- **Typography:** Editorial pairing of `Playfair Display` serif headlines and `Plus Jakarta Sans` geometric sans-serif body.
- **Palette:** Sovereign 22K Gold (`#775a19` / `#c5a059`), Royal Emerald (`#3e6752`), and warm Atelier Cream (`#fbf9f6`).
- **Icons:** Google Material Symbols Outlined.

### 2. Female-Centric Sensory & Motion Experience
- **Interactive Acoustic Bangle Chime (Khanak):** Pure harmonic gold resonance synthesis using the Web Audio API with synchronized animated soundwave bars.
- **Delicate Gemstone Sparkles:** Animated golden starlight glints (`.animate-sparkle`) overlaid on the masterwork kada and customizer canvas.
- **Atmospheric Lighting Simulation:** Dynamic ambient lighting switchers (*Studio Glow*, *Sunlit Pavilion*, *Candlelight Noir*) to simulate how polki diamonds reflect light in different settings.
- **Gold Shimmer & Foil Hover:** Subtle luxury light sweep on primary CTAs (`.animate-shimmer`) and gold foil hover states (`.gold-foil-hover`).

### 3. Bespoke 3D Bangle Customizer Studio
- **Dynamic 3D & Front SVG Visualizer:** Live rendering of bangle stacks (solitary kadas, auspicious pairs, festive sets, and 14-piece bridal choodas) with realistic gold shaders, silk/velvet wraps, uncut polki diamonds, and hanging latkans/ghungroos.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Interactive perspective view toggle (`Stack View` vs `Front Ring`), shimmer toggle, and live price re-computation.
- **Personalization:** Custom secret inner contour monogram inscriptions, heirloom cipher engravings, and velvet presentation chests.

### 4. Precision Mandrel Sizing Studio
- **Interactive Millimeter Slider:** Real-time bangle diameter finder from 52mm (2-2) to 68mm (2-10) with automatic wrist fit detection.
- **Measurement Methodologies:** Knuckle span measuring tape guidelines and traditional Indian bangle chart conversion table.

### 5. Multi-Currency & Seamless WhatsApp Commerce
- **Live Currency Selector:** Instant switching between `USD ($)`, `EUR (€)`, `GBP (£)`, and `INR (₹)`.
- **Direct WhatsApp Concierge:** One-click order transmission directly to WhatsApp pre-formatted with item breakdown, gold purity, sizes, custom engravings, and transit protocols.
- **Quick View Modal:** Full product exploration modal without leaving the catalog feed.
